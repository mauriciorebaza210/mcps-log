Service Day Changed — Email
Sent automatically whenever a customer's promised service day or technician changes. This is what lets the welcome email state a day with confidence.

Why this email exists

The welcome email tells the customer “Your service day is Wednesday.” That promise has to stay true — but today a pool can be moved on the route board and the customer is never told.

This closes that gap: any real change to a customer who was already told a day triggers this automatically. Customers who were never given a day (startups, green-to-cleans) get nothing, and a one-week shuffle doesn't trigger it either — only a permanent change does.

When this does not send

One-week shuffles. Moving someone for a single week (holiday, weather) uses the existing day-before and on-my-way texts — this email is only for permanent changes.

Customers never told a day. Startups and green-to-cleans don't get a promised day in their welcome email, so there's no promise to correct.

When you turn it off. The route board's move dialog has a “tell the customer” toggle, default on — flip it off for a correction the customer shouldn't be bothered with.
