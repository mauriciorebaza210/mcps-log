Voice decisions on this screen

Readings show “In range” under the number, and the plain-language line says what the set of them means together. The voice rules are explicit that “free chlorine at 1.2” with no context is jargon that erodes trust.

The technician's note is attributed by name. A named person is what makes it feel like a service rather than a system.

“We'll text when he's on the way” is a promise the portal already keeps — HeadsUp.js sends that email today.

No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hedging. Nothing says “should be fine.”

Under itThe data boundary and the security posture

This is the first surface on the portal that anyone outside the company can reach, so what it can and cannot see is the design.

The customer can see
  • Their own visits, for one pool only
  • Photos from those visits
  • The six chemistry readings, paired with plain-language meaning
  • Actions performed and equipment status
  • The customer-facing Notes field
  • Their next scheduled visit and technician
Never reachable
  • Internal Notes — a separate column, never in the response
  • Chemical cost, margin, or any payroll figure
  • The internal risk score or LSI from sketch 1
  • Any other customer's pool, by any means
  • Route data, technician schedules, or staffing
  • Anything at all without a valid, unexpired token
Scoped to one poolA token carries exactly one pool_id. There is no parameter a customer can change to reach another.
Hashed at restOnly a SHA-256 hash is stored in the Customer_Sessions sheet. The raw token exists only in the customer's link.
30 days, revocableExpiry is enforced server-side, and flipping one cell kills a link immediately.
Read-only, with one exceptionThe service request is the only write, and it goes into a sheet you already monitor.
Rate limitedPer token and per IP, on both issuance and validation, in the Vercel proxy layer.
Whitelist, not blacklistThe GAS handler names the fields it returns. A new column added to the sheet later cannot leak by default.
One thing to decide before this ships. Visit photos are currently saved to Drive as ANYONE_WITH_LINK. The URLs are long and unguessable, so this is not an open door — but it does mean a photo URL forwarded by anyone stays viewable. If you want them locked to the portal, that's a change to saveVisitPhotos_ and worth deciding now rather than after customers have links.

Where it touches the code: a new Customer_Sessions sheet; a new appscript/CustomerPortal.js reusing buildVisitReportPayload_() rather than re-deriving the visit shape; a new api/portal/ proxy following the api/agreement/sign.js pattern; a standalone portal.html like agreement.html, outside the SPA; one button appended in VisitReportEmail.js; one rewrite in vercel.json.
