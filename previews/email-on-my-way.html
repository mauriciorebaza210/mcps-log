"On My Way" Email — 3 directions
A short heads-up the customer receives the moment their technician taps "On My Way" in the portal.
Today this moment is handled by text message — the portal sends an internal email to Mau/Antonio with a pre-filled SMS link they tap to text the customer. There is no customer-facing email for this yet. These three mockups design that new email as a companion to (or replacement for) the SMS, using the same "on the way" wording already approved in the brand voice guide.
