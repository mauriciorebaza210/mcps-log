What you're looking at

A proposed redesign of the admin side of the MCPS portal — the dashboard the office sees on login, and the Admin page behind it. It's a mockup: none of it is built, and the data shown is invented to test the layout under realistic names and numbers.

It's drawn to your brand guide, not to what the portal looks like today. The live portal runs Oswald and Barlow at teal #0d4d44 with a gold accent. This uses Montserrat and Open Sans at Primary Teal #0D3D3E with Aqua #1FA7A8, and no gold. The Brand tab lays out that gap and what it would take to close it.

Two things need your call, both on the Brand tab: status colors (the palette has no urgent / warning / good, and an operations queue needs them — three muted options are proposed), and a white horizontal logo (the guide asks for it on teal, but no clean transparent white file exists in the repo, so the sidebar uses the full-color lockup on a white band as the compliant fallback).

Admin dashboard — replaces the five-row layout in loadHomeStats(). Four business KPIs, then three operational counters, then the Action Center. Every number comes from a source the page already fetches. The three "Coming Soon" cards, the hardcoded activity feed, and every --- are gone. Styled to the official brand: Montserrat headings, Open Sans body, Primary Teal anchor, Aqua accent.

Two things need your decision

1. The portal doesn't currently use the brand. Today it runs Oswald + Barlow at teal #0d4d44 with a gold accent #c8a84b. The brand guide specifies Montserrat + Open Sans at #0D3D3E with Aqua #1FA7A8, and gold isn't in the palette at all. This mockup follows the guide. That means the renewed admin screens will look different from the rest of the portal until the other pages follow — see the options below.

2. Status colors aren't in the palette. An ops queue needs urgent / warning / good. The guide says to flag rather than invent, so the three below are proposals, chosen to sit quietly next to teal and aqua. Say the word and I'll change or drop them.

Alternative if you'd rather add nothing: encode severity with position and weight only — urgent items sort to the top with a Charcoal badge, everything else stays gray. It's calmer, but slower to scan.

Typography
Montserrat 700 · caps Page title
Montserrat 700 · caps Panel title / section label
Montserrat 700 142 KPI values and all figures
Montserrat 700 · caps Button label Short words only
Open Sans 600 Row titles and emphasis in tables
Open Sans 400 Body copy, metric labels, table cells, empty states

ALL CAPS is applied at page-title and panel-title level only, per the brand rule. Body copy is never capitalised. Loaded from Google Fonts: Montserrat:wght@600;700 and Open+Sans:wght@400;600;700.

Derived neutrals — not new brand colors
Borders — Primary Teal at 12% alphargba(13,61,62,.12)
Inner dividers — Primary Teal at 7% alphargba(13,61,62,.07)
Muted text — desaturated teal-gray#5F6B6C
"Medium" badge text — Aqua darkened for contrast on its own tint#14706F

Light Gray #F3F5F6 is almost invisible as a 1px border on white, so borders use Primary Teal at low alpha instead. That keeps everything inside the palette rather than introducing a seventh color.

Logo
Sidebar — Horizontal, full color, on a white bandIn use
Mobile top bar — same asset, knocked out to whiteTemporary

The guide calls for the white logo variant on Primary Teal, but there's no clean transparent white horizontal PNG or SVG in assets/ — the only white file, White Logo Words.png, has a baked-in dark background and carries a "Clean Pools. Done Right." tagline. So the sidebar puts the approved full-color horizontal logo on a white band, which is the compliant fallback, and the mobile bar uses a CSS filter as a stopgap. Worth asking Tony to export a white horizontal SVG — then both become exact.

How far should the brand roll out?
A. Admin surfaces only — scoped CSS, admin looks brand-correct, rest of the portal unchangedSmall
B. Retoken the whole portal — swap the six values in :root and the font links; everything shifts at onceMedium
C. Keep the portal's current look — rebuild this mockup on #0d4d44 + gold insteadSmall

B is the honest answer if the goal is one consistent company. It's a bigger blast radius — every page, every hardcoded hex in the feature files — so it deserves its own pass rather than riding along with the admin work.
