The mechanicWhy a pool on a paid weekly route still turns green

This is the part that needs your sign-off before anything ships, so it comes first.

Chlorine is not measured against a fixed number. It is measured against stabilizer.

Cyanuric acid protects chlorine from burning off in the sun, but it also binds it. The share of chlorine actually free to sanitize tracks the FC-to-CYA ratio, not the FC number by itself. Tab feeders add CYA continuously and it never leaves the water except by dilution — so a pool on tabs drifts upward all season, and the chlorine target has to rise with it.

minimum effective FC = CYA × 0.075 salt / SWG pools: CYA × 0.05 target FC band = CYA × 0.075 → CYA × 0.15 shock threshold = CYA × 0.40

The mechanicWhether the water is eating the plaster or coating the heater

The second calculation, using three readings already collected on every visit.

Langelier Saturation Index

LSI says which direction the water is pushing. Positive deposits scale on tile, plumbing and heater elements. Negative dissolves plaster and corrodes metal. Neither shows up in a single reading — both show up as a pool that sits slightly off for two months.

LSI = pH + TF + CF + AF − 12.1 TF temperature factor ← the only input not currently logged CF calcium hardness factor AF carbonate alkalinity factor (TA corrected for CYA) balanced: −0.3 … +0.3

pH, CH and TA are already on every visit. Temperature is handled as you chose: a new optional Water Temp field in the Test Results step, and when it's left blank the value is estimated from the Open-Meteo forecast the route page already fetches — with the readout labelled est. so nobody mistakes it for a measurement. An estimated LSI is more than accurate enough to catch a pool sitting at +0.6 for two months quietly scaling the tile line.

What changed on this screen

① The LOW / IDEAL / HIGH pill above FC is no longer fixed at 3–5. It reads against this pool's CYA, so it says Below min at 1.5 ppm instead of Low.

② The target callout explains the number rather than just flagging it, and offers the dose. Use this writes into the Treat step through the existing _svcApplyTreatRecs_ prefill, which already refuses to overwrite anything the tech typed themselves.

③ The CYA trend line is the tell that the tab feeder is the root cause, not the tech.

④ Water Temp is optional and pre-filled from the forecast.

⑤ The Water balance panel stays collapsed until pH, TA and CH are all entered — it can't compute without them.

Under itThe scoring model, in full

One pass over the last 8 visits per pool. Weights add up; the total picks the band. Printed here so you can argue with the numbers before they ship — all of them live in one tunable block.

● At risk — 50 and aboveSomething is actively wrong. Goes to the Home card and the top of the radar.
◐ Watch — 25 to 49Drifting. Worth a look before it becomes a callback.
○ Healthy — under 25Collapsed by default. No noise.

Every signal that fires returns a human-readable sentence, which is what the cards display. There is no screen anywhere that shows a bare score with no explanation. Dismiss 30 days exists so a pool you have already decided about stops nagging — for instance one that is scheduled for a drain next month.
